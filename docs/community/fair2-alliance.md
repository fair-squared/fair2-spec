# FAIR² Alliance

The **FAIR² Alliance** is the neutral, non-profit governing body responsible for stewarding the FAIR² Open Specification. Registered as a Swiss association, the Alliance ensures that the FAIR² standard evolves through transparent, community-driven processes, enabling AI-ready, ethically governed, and FAIR-compliant data ecosystems.

## Purpose

The FAIR² Alliance exists to:

- Ensure **neutral governance** of the FAIR² specification
- Maintain **transparency** in technical and policy decisions
- Safeguard the **long-term sustainability and interoperability** of FAIR²
- Promote **trust, openness, and accountability** in AI-ready FAIR data management
- Support adoption across domains (science, health, public data, industry)

## Legal Structure

- **Type:** Swiss non-profit association (Verein)
- **Jurisdiction:** Switzerland
- **Scope:** Global, open participation
- **Status:** Independent, vendor-neutral, community-led

## Roles and Responsibilities

### 1. **Stewardship of the Specification**
- Approve and version official FAIR² releases
- Validate compatibility with Schema.org, DCAT, DataCite, ODPS, ODRL, ML-Schema
- Maintain public JSON-LD contexts and SHACL shapes

### 2. **Working Groups**
The Alliance coordinates open, collaborative working groups:

| Group | Scope |
|-------|-------|
| **Specification WG** | Evolves the metadata model and vocabulary |
| **Policy & Access WG** | Defines agreement levels, licenses, ODRL policies |
| **Implementation WG** | Supports adopters, validators, and reference tooling |
| **Outreach WG** | Coordinates documentation, training, and engagement |

### 3. **Certification & Licensing**
- Define FAIR² compliance criteria
- Maintain and publish certification checklists and templates
- Grant use of the FAIR² trademark for:
  - Certified datasets and portals
  - Non-commercial, non-competitive educational use

## Decision-Making Model

The Alliance follows an open and inclusive governance process:

- **Consensus-driven**: Technical decisions made collaboratively by working groups
- **Public roadmap**: Priorities and changes published and openly discussed
- **Transparency**: Meeting notes, proposals, and discussions are documented and archived
- **Voting**: Formal proposals may be approved by majority vote when consensus fails

## Membership

The Alliance is open to:

- Individual contributors
- Institutional stakeholders (research orgs, publishers, data custodians)
- Domain-specific initiatives aligned with FAIR² goals

Members may:
- Join working groups
- Submit proposals or feedback
- Nominate representatives for coordination roles

## Compliance and Licensing

The FAIR² Alliance:
- Maintains a public license registry (ODRL policies, agreement levels)
- Publishes a reference SHACL vocabulary for dataset validation
- Supports organizations in:
  - Certifying FAIR²-compliant metadata
  - Embedding machine-readable usage policies
  - Aligning access levels to ethical and regulatory expectations

## Funding and Sustainability

FAIR² is sustained through:
- Institutional support from data infrastructure stakeholders
- Grant funding for open standard development
- Voluntary contributions and in-kind work by members
- Future: nominal fees for certification services or training (non-profit basis)
