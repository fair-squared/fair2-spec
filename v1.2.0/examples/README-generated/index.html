    Borja_2025_Marine_Biodiversity_and_Environmental_Data_Dataset

    Overview

    This dataset provides 28 years of environmental monitoring data (1995–2023) from 51 stations in the estuaries and coastal areas of the Basque Country in the Bay of Biscay. Covering 130 variables across water, sediments, and biota, it includes detailed records of phytoplankton, macroalgae, macroinvertebrates, and fish. Compiled in collaboration with the Basque Water Agency (URA), the dataset supports analysis of environmental responses to human pressures and management interventions, identifying patterns of environmental quality improvement within a context of natural variability. Provided in a FAIR² Data Package, the dataset is structured to ensure Findability, Accessibility, Interoperability, Reusability, and Artificial Intelligence-readiness, facilitated by structured metadata and Application Programming Interface access. This resource is intended for researchers and policymakers examining anthropogenic and natural influences on aquatic ecosystems, contributing to sustainable management, conservation practices, and alignment with European directives, including the Water Framework Directive.

    Contents of this package

    File Format URL Description
    AUTHORS.csv text/csv https://sen.science/doi/10.71728/r1rj-f947/resources/record-sets/AUTHORS.csv Contains information about authors, including their affiliations, contact details, and institutional information, primarily from AZTI in Spain.
    METHOD.csv text/csv https://sen.science/doi/10.71728/r1rj-f947/resources/record-sets/METHOD.csv Describes sampling protocols for various taxa groups, offering insights into environmental monitoring methods.
    FISH.csv text/csv https://sen.science/doi/10.71728/r1rj-f947/resources/record-sets/FISH.csv Contains data on fish abundance, with site details and taxonomic information, categorized under human observation records.
    INVERTEBRATES.csv text/csv https://sen.science/doi/10.71728/r1rj-f947/resources/record-sets/INVERTEBRATES.csv Documents invertebrate abundance across sites, including metadata such as depth and sampling effort, with a focus on abundance values.
    MACROALGAE.csv text/csv https://sen.science/doi/10.71728/r1rj-f947/resources/record-sets/MACROALGAE.csv Details macroalgae coverage, recorded as human observations with taxonomic data and coverage percentages.
    PHYTOPLANKTON.csv text/csv https://sen.science/doi/10.71728/r1rj-f947/resources/record-sets/PHYTOPLANKTON.csv Records phytoplankton distribution with taxonomic information and depth measurements, facilitating ecological studies.
    SEDIMENTS.csv text/csv https://sen.science/doi/10.71728/r1rj-f947/resources/record-sets/SEDIMENTS.csv Captures sediment data, including organic matter percentage and metal concentrations, providing insights into sediment composition.
    BIOTA.csv text/csv https://sen.science/doi/10.71728/r1rj-f947/resources/record-sets/BIOTA.csv Details collection dates, locations, and species names, focusing on environmental parameters and biotic composition.
    WATER.csv text/csv https://sen.science/doi/10.71728/r1rj-f947/resources/record-sets/WATER.csv Includes measurements of parameters like temperature, salinity, and pH across various sites, with metadata such as collection dates and site IDs.
    METADATA.csv text/csv https://sen.science/doi/10.71728/r1rj-f947/resources/record-sets/METADATA.csv Encompasses data on taxonomic groups, sampling methods, and site-specific identifiers, providing an overview of aquatic biodiversity research.
    READ+ME.csv text/csv https://sen.science/doi/10.71728/r1rj-f947/resources/record-sets/READ+ME.csv
    ANALYTICAL_METHODS.csv text/csv https://sen.science/doi/10.71728/r1rj-f947/resources/record-sets/ANALYTICAL_METHODS.csv Contains analysis methods used in the experiment.
    notebook.ipynb application/x-ipynb+json https://sen.science/doi/10.71728/r1rj-f947/notebook.ipynb Contains an example Jupyter notebook to explore the dataset.
